Centralized Counselling & Samarth Portals Directory

Every participating university runs its own admission registration. Missing a university’s individual deadline disqualifies you from their regular rounds. Find direct application links, counselling schedules, and critical certificate deadlines below.

warning

Crucial Rule: Central List vs. State Certificate Validity

For admission to Central Universities (DU, JNU, BHU, TISS, SAU), State-level OBC certificates are not accepted. Your caste MUST be present in the Central List of OBCs published by the National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC). Furthermore, your OBC-NCL or EWS certificate must be issued on or after March 31, 2024 (for current session) or March 31, 2025 (for the upcoming academic cycle).

Central Deemed University

Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS)

Campuses: Mumbai, Hyderabad, Guwahati, Tuljapur

COQP12 & COQP11

All PG admissions at TISS require registering on the official TISS PG Admissions portal. Cutoff evaluations for MBA/HRM flagships are conducted strictly via COQP12 raw scores. Social work, public health, and policy streams evaluate via COQP11.

Counselling Platform:TISS Online Application System (OAMS)
Rounds:Round 1, Round 2, Waitlist & Spot
Interview Stage:Direct Merit (No GD/PI for CUET PG)

The 5-Step CUET PG Counselling Roadmap

Follow this strict workflow to ensure your application is recognized by university selection committees.

1

NTA Scorecard Release

Download official scorecard with Raw Marks and Shift Percentiles.

2

Individual Registration

Register on each target university's Samarth portal (TISS, DU, JNU, BHU).

3

Preference Locking

Order your desired programs in priority sequence before the deadline.

4

Seat Allocation & Fee

If allotted, pay provisional admission fee within 48-72 hours to secure seat.

5

Spot & Mop-Up Rounds

Participate in open spot rounds for leftover vacant seats at discounted cutoffs.
