COQP12 Most Competitive Paper

MBA, General Management, HRM & Tourism

Accepted By: TISS Mumbai (HRM/ODCL), SAU (MBA), IIIT Lucknow, BHU FMS, JNU ABVS

COQP12 evaluates comprehensive business and management aptitude without subject-specific engineering or finance prerequisites. It consists of four core mathematical and analytical modules:

calculate 1. Quantitative Ability & Mathematics (~25 Questions)

  • Arithmetic: Percentages, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Ratio & Proportion
  • Time, Speed & Distance, Work & Wages, Pipes & Cisterns
  • Algebra: Linear & Quadratic Equations, Progressions (AP/GP)
  • Geometry & Mensuration: Triangles, Circles, Coordinate basics, Volumes

table_chart 2. Data Interpretation (~15 Questions)

  • Tabular Data, Bar Charts, Multiple Bar Graphs
  • Pie Charts & Degree-to-Percentage Conversions
  • Line Graphs & Trend Interpolations
  • Caselet DI & Venn Diagram-based Data Sets

psychology 3. Logical & Analytical Reasoning (~20 Questions)

  • Linear & Circular Seating Arrangements, Blood Relations
  • Syllogisms, Statement & Assumption, Course of Action
  • Coding-Decoding, Direction Sense, Series Completion
  • Puzzles, Scheduling matrices, and Ranking tests

menu_book 4. Verbal Ability & English Comprehension (~15 Questions)

  • Reading Comprehension Passages (Inference & Direct)
  • Vocabulary: Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, One-word substitutes
  • Grammar: Error Spotting, Sentence Correction, Subject-Verb Agreement
  • Para Jumbles & Sentence Rearrangement

11
COQP11 General & Professional Paper

General Paper: LL.B, Social Work, Public Policy & Public Health

Accepted By: DU Faculty of Law (LL.B), TISS Campuses (20+ MA Streams), BHU, JNU, Central Universities

COQP11 is the primary examination gate for legal studies, social policy, urban management, library science, and developmental programs. It features heavy emphasis on General Knowledge, Computer Awareness, and Analytical Aptitude.

public 1. General Knowledge & Current Affairs (~20 Questions)

  • Indian Polity, Constitution & Fundamental Rights
  • Modern Indian History, Geography & Environment
  • National Socio-Economic Schemes, UN Bodies & Treaties
  • Recent Supreme Court Landmark Judgments (vital for DU LL.B)

devices 2. Computer Basics & Digital Literacy (~15 Questions)

  • Computer Hardware Architecture, Memory (RAM, ROM, Cache)
  • Operating Systems, File Management, Shortcuts
  • Networking, Internet Protocols, Cybersecurity basics
  • MS Office productivity tools (Word, Excel formulas, PowerPoint)

translate 3. Language Comprehension (English or Hindi) (~20 Questions)

  • Unseen Passages & Critical Reading
  • Vocabulary, Synonyms, Antonyms, Analogies
  • Sentence Improvement, Prepositions & Tenses
  • Idiomatic expressions & Contextual usage

balance 4. General Aptitude & Logical Reasoning (~20 Questions)

  • Basic Quantitative Math: Number Systems, Averages, Percentages
  • Logical Deduction, Assertion-Reason, Cause & Effect
  • Analogies, Odd-man-out, Number & Letter series
  • Venn diagrams and basic geometric reasoning

SCQP09 Science & Computing Paper

Master of Computer Applications (MCA) & Data Science

Accepted By: JNU (SC&IS), DU (Dept of CS), BHU, Central University of Punjab, IIIT-L (Data Analytics)

High-rigor computing exam testing engineering-level mathematics and fundamental computer science principles. Essential for admission into prestigious research institutions.

functions 1. Higher Mathematics & Algebra (~35 Questions)

  • Set Theory, Relations & Functions, Mathematical Induction
  • Matrices & Determinants, System of Linear Equations
  • Coordinate Geometry, Straight Lines, Conic Sections
  • Differential & Integral Calculus: Limits, Continuity, Definite Integrals, Maxima-Minima
  • Probability Distributions (Binomial, Poisson) & Statistics

terminal 2. Computer Architecture & Data Structures (~40 Questions)

  • Digital Logic: Boolean Algebra, Karnaugh Maps, Logic Gates, Flip-Flops
  • Number Systems: Binary, Octal, Hexadecimal conversions, 1's and 2's Complement
  • C/C++ Programming: Syntax, Pointers, Recursion, Object-Oriented principles
  • Data Structures: Arrays, Stacks, Queues, Linked Lists, Trees, Graph Traversals
  • Operating Systems Basics: CPU Scheduling, Deadlocks, Paging & Virtual Memory
