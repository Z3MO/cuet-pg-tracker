Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At CUET PG Tracker, accessible from https://cuetpgtracker.me, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document explains what information is collected, how it is processed, and our commitment to student data protection.

1. Information We Collect & Client-Side Processing

CUET PG Tracker operates as a lightweight client-side application. We do NOT require user account creation, registration, or submission of personally identifiable contact information (e.g. phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses) to use our tools.

  • Client-Side Score Calculations: Expected scores, category selections, and paper codes entered into the Admissions Predictor are processed entirely inside your device’s browser. Your inputs are not transmitted to or stored in any remote private database.
  • Standard Log & Analytics Data: Like most web portals, we utilize Google Analytics (GA4) to understand traffic patterns and usage metrics. This includes anonymous metrics like IP addresses, browser types, session durations, and page paths.

2. Cookies & Advertising Partners

Our website displays third-party advertisements via Google AdSense. Third-party ad vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

3. Public Government Allotment Data

All student merit list allotment records displayed in the Category Merit Explorer are derived from publicly released institutional counselling result registers published by authorized universities (TISS, SAU, IIIT Lucknow). No private, unreleased student data is accessed or stored.

4. Consent

By utilizing CUET PG Tracker, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its provisions.
