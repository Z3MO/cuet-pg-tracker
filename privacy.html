Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

At CUET PG Tracker, accessible from https://cuetpgtracker.me, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by CUET PG Tracker and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

CUET PG Tracker operates as a static client-side web application. We do not require you to register, log in, or provide personal identification details (such as names, phone numbers, or emails) to use our rank predictor or browser tools.

  • Anonymous CUET Marks: The marks, category, and paper codes you input into the Rank Predictor tool are processed entirely locally in your browser. We do not store this data on our servers.
  • Log Files & Analytics: Like most websites, we collect anonymous visitor analytics via third-party tools such as Google Analytics. This includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click paths to analyze trends and user engagement.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

CUET PG Tracker uses cookies to save configuration preferences (such as your active Dark/Light theme selection) to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are also utilized by third-party services as detailed below.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Ezoic

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.

Similarly, Ezoic and other advertising partners use advanced machine learning cookies to test layout densities, customize ad targeting, and audit revenue payouts. These systems track anonymous usage patterns across views.

These third-party ad servers use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on CUET PG Tracker, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.
