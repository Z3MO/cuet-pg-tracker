Crucial Counselling Advisory for Indian Aspirants

CUET PG seat allotments do not occur through a single centralized national counselling window. Every Central, Deemed, and State University operates an independent counselling portal on the Samarth eGov infrastructure.

01

Raw Score vs Normalised Percentile

For single-shift papers (e.g. COQP12), universities evaluate based purely on the NTA Raw Score (out of 300). Multi-shift exams use equipercentile normalisation.

02

Caste Certificate Validity Window

OBC-NCL and EWS certificates MUST be issued on or after March 31, 2024 in official Central Government format. State OBC certificates are not accepted.

03

CSAS & Spot Round Procedures

Missing a university registration deadline bars you from regular rounds. However, vacant seats are published during Open Spot Rounds where cutoffs often drop significantly.
