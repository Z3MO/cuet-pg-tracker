Frequently Asked Questions (CUET PG Admissions)

Find answers to common queries regarding safe scores, percentiles, and university counseling criteria.

Q. What is a safe score for TISS Mumbai in CUET PG 2026?

For the flagship HRM & LR and Analytics programs (COQP12), a score of 235+ is considered highly safe for General category candidates. For COQP11 programs (Social Work, Public Health, and policy streams), a score of 180+ is generally safe.

Q. Which paper codes does South Asian University (SAU) accept for MBA?

SAU accepts multiple paper codes for its MBA program: COQP12 (General Management), COQP10 (Commerce), and COQP08 (Economics). Candidates who wrote multiple exams are ranked in the merit list based on their highest score among these paper codes.

Q. How are the estimated percentiles calculated in the tracker?

Percentiles are calculated using a data-driven piecewise linear interpolation formula. This formula is derived directly from the official NTA baseline ranking databases, mapping raw scores to their corresponding percentile rankings for each paper code (COQP11, COQP12, COQP10, and COQP08).

Q. Can I get admission to SAU without writing the CUET PG exam?

Yes, South Asian University (SAU) reserves seats for candidates entering through their own separate entrance examination (**SAU-ET**). In our tracker, these candidates are registered with a score status of "N/A" and the exam code "SAU-ET".
